NHS GP Prescribing of Sleep Medications: Z drugs and Benzodiazepines (2020–2025) Using OpenPrescribing (Continued)

(November 2025)

OpenPrescribing

Further examination of the downloaded prescribing data for Temazepam is presented below (Table, Figure 1): Between September 2020 and May 2022, prescription items (mean: 60k, SD: 4.1k), quantity (1,571k, SD:119k), and spending (£145k, SD:20k) across England showed a gradual decline. Beginning in June 2022, items and quantity dropped sharply, reaching their lowest levels in December 2022 (items:16k; quantity:463k). They then increased and remained relatively stable until August 2025, with average values of 27k items and 721k quantity for the period January 2023 to August 2025. Spending, however, followed a different pattern. Starting in June 2022, it rose dramatically to a peak of £1,112k in September, before declining through December. It then fluctuated briefly before stabilising from March 2023 until August 2025. The period from June 2022 to February 2023 is particularly notable: while prescribing items and quantity fell sharply, spending increased significantly (costs more than tripled) - an interesting divergence in trends.
