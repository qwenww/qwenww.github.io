NHS GP Prescribing of Sleep Medications: Z drugs and Benzodiazepines (2020–2025) Using OpenPrescribing (Continued)

(November 2025)

OpenPrescribing

Further examination of the downloaded prescribing data for Temazepam, as demonstrated in the following figure (Figure 1), revealed a positive association between the number of prescribed items and the total quantity, while spending showed an inverse relationship with both during the period from September 2020 to October 2022.

In sensitivity analyses restricted to Z-drugs, the South West consistently exhibited the highest prescribing rate, whereas London continued to have the lowest across the period 2020–2025 (Figure 2).
